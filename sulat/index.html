Keys and interactions

R/Q: rainforest. L/W: temperate forest. N/K: taiga. E/T: water. J/P: cold water. U/F: reef. I/Y: estuary. O: swamp. V: ice. S/B: savanna. C/G: steppe. Z/M: tundra. A/D: sand. X: mountain. H: plain. Space leaves a gap.

Water touching forest can become estuary. Rivers and lakes turn nearby grass into marsh. Estuaries and coasts turn nearby forest into mangrove. Flowers spread into grass; water cools lava into rock. Contact must persist across steps.

Forest patches release birds. Deer and foxes move through woodland and open ground, goats through rocky terrain, and crabs through sand, coast, marsh, and mangrove. Water, spaces, and lava break land routes. Ecology controls biome changes and animal births.

Translation rewrites recognized words and syllables. Dotted underlines mark rewritten text; solid underlines mark biome changes. The active line stays editable. Pause holds movement and mutations.
